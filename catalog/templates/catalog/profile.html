Why this profile matters

Metabolic context

Height, weight, age, gender, and lifestyle are used to estimate BMI, BMR, and TDEE. Those values are passed to the LLM so recipe advice can be aligned with maintenance, loss, or gain goals.

Culture and cuisine

Culture and cuisine do not change BMR or TDEE, but they do make the suggested dishes more realistic, familiar, and useful.

Safety and fit

Diet style, fasting, allergies, and exclusions are applied before recipe generation so the planner avoids suggesting something incompatible.

Admin-managed accounts

Only superusers create accounts. There is no public registration flow, so profile ownership stays controlled from the admin panel.
